## Overview

A client-commissioned UI/UX case study for Sultan's Dine — reimagining how a premium restaurant brand presents itself in the digital space. The project explored the intersection of brand storytelling and functional food ordering, delivering a design system that feels as refined as the dining experience itself.

## Key Contributions

- **Brand-First Design System** — Developed a cohesive visual language that translates Sultan's Dine's premium identity into every screen, from splash to checkout.
- **Menu Experience Design** — Designed rich, editorial-style menu layouts with appetite-driven imagery and intuitive categorization that makes browsing feel like discovery.
- **End-to-End User Flow** — Mapped and designed the complete customer journey — reservation, ordering, payment, and feedback — into a single seamless experience.
- **Client Presentation & Handoff** — Delivered polished prototypes with detailed design specifications ready for development handoff.
