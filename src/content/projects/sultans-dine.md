## Overview

A focused UI case study for Sultan's Dine, designing a seamless food delivery experience tailored for a single restaurant brand. The project explored how to balance brand identity with usability in a food ordering context.

## Key Design Decisions

- **Brand Integration** — Incorporated Sultan's Dine's brand identity throughout the app while maintaining usability standards.
- **Streamlined Ordering** — Designed a simplified ordering flow that minimizes steps from menu browsing to checkout.
- **Menu Presentation** — Created visually rich menu layouts with high-quality imagery and clear categorization.
