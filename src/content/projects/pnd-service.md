## Overview

PND Service is the supply chain backbone for Bangladesh's booming Facebook marketplace economy. A B2B wholesale platform built for small business pages that run their entire operation on social media — PND lets them source products, manage inventory, and get orders delivered to customers, all without putting up any capital. Zero budget, zero risk, full business.

## Key Contributions

- **Seller-First Platform Design** — Built an experience tailored for Facebook-native sellers who may not have traditional e-commerce experience, prioritizing simplicity and speed.
- **Order & Fulfillment Pipeline** — Developed end-to-end order management from wholesale sourcing through last-mile delivery coordination.
- **Frontend Development** — Designed and implemented the platform UI in React.js with a focus on clarity, fast load times, and mobile responsiveness.
- **API Integration** — Connected frontend interfaces with backend services for real-time inventory sync, pricing updates, and order tracking.
