## Overview

Grade Limited is a B2B e-commerce and digitalization platform designed to streamline business-to-business transactions and digital transformation processes. The platform provides a comprehensive suite of tools for procurement, inventory management, and digital commerce.

## Key Contributions

- **E-Commerce Platform** — Built a robust B2B e-commerce platform with features tailored for business buyers and sellers.
- **Digital Transformation** — Implemented digital tools to help businesses transition from traditional to digital operations.
- **Scalable Architecture** — Designed a modular system architecture that supports scaling with growing business needs.
