## Overview

Grade Bangladesh flips the script on corporate procurement. A B2B e-commerce platform where businesses empower their employees to purchase daily essentials — groceries, household goods, personal care — at the lowest possible prices, all subsidized through their employer. Think of it as an employee benefit that actually gets used.

## Key Contributions

- **Platform Architecture** — Designed and built a scalable multi-tenant system where each business gets a tailored storefront for their employees.
- **Pricing Engine** — Developed a dynamic pricing layer that surfaces the best deals by aggregating supplier rates in real time.
- **Employee Portal** — Built an intuitive shopping experience that feels consumer-grade despite being a B2B tool — because nobody should need training to buy toothpaste.
- **Admin & Analytics Dashboard** — Delivered business-facing dashboards for tracking spend, managing budgets, and monitoring employee engagement.
